## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, Schengen visa rules, and weather tracking.

- [Switzerland Tourism (Official Portal - MySwitzerland)](https://www.myswitzerland.com/) - Official national tourist board guide featuring destination highlights, alpine itineraries, and regional guides.
- [State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) - Entry Requirements](https://www.sem.admin.ch/) - Official government portal for checking Schengen visa regulations, passport rules, and entry guidelines.
- [MeteoSwiss (Federal Office of Meteorology and Climatology)](https://www.meteoswiss.ch/) - Official national meteorological service providing precise weather forecasts, severe weather warnings, and high-altitude climate radar.

## Transportation & Swiss Travel System

Navigating panoramic scenic trains, integrated public transit, mountain railways, and boat networks.

- [Swiss Federal Railways (SBB CFF FFS)](https://www.sbb.ch/en) - Official national railway operator managing intercity train lines across Switzerland.
- [Grand Train Tour of Switzerland (MySwitzerland)](https://www.myswitzerland.com/) - Official scenic rail route planner connecting iconic journeys like the Glacier Express and Bernina Express.
- [Swiss International Air Lines (SWISS)](https://www.swiss.com/) - National flag carrier operating international flights through Zürich Airport (ZRH) and Geneva Airport (GVA).

## Destinations & Regions

### Zürich & Northeastern Switzerland

- [Zürich Tourism (Official City Portal)](https://www.zuerich.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the historic Altstadt, Lake Zürich promenades, and world-class museums.

### Bern, Jungfrau & Bernese Oberland

- [Bern Welcome (Official Capital Portal)](https://www.bern.com/) - Official visitor guide for the UNESCO-listed medieval old town, Rose Garden, and Parliament building.
- [Jungfrau Region Tourism Portal](https://www.jungfrau.ch/) - Official travel guide for exploring Grindelwald, Lauterbrunnen, and the Jungfraujoch (Top of Europe) railway.

### Lucerne & Central Switzerland

- [Lucerne Tourism (Lucerne.com)](https://www.luzern.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the Chapel Bridge, Lake Lucerne, and surrounding peaks (Mount Pilatus, Rigi).

### Geneva & Lake Geneva Region

- [Geneva Tourism (Geneva.com)](https://www.geneve.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for Lake Geneva, the Jet d'Eau, international quarters, and historic old town.
- [Montreux Riviera Tourism](https://www.montreuxriviera.com/) - Regional portal for exploring Chillon Castle, lakeside promenades, and Lavaux UNESCO vineyards.

### Zermatt & Valais Alps

- [Zermatt Tourism (Matterhorn Region)](https://www.zermatt.ch/) - Official visitor portal for exploring the Matterhorn, Gornergrat railway, and car-free alpine village.

### Graubünden & St. Moritz

- [Graubünden Tourism Portal](https://www.graubuenden.ch/) - Official regional travel hub for exploring St. Moritz, Swiss National Park, and alpine valleys.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, medical assistance, and mountain safety protocols.

- [Emergency Helpline (Police 117 / Ambulance 144 / Fire 118)](https://www.ch.ch/) - Direct national emergency dispatch numbers operational across all cantons.
- [REGA (Swiss Air-Rescue)](https://www.rega.ch/) - National air rescue service providing emergency medical evacuation in remote alpine and mountainous terrain.
